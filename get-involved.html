Volunteer with DYC / JSC

Share your time, skills, and ideas to support Deaf youth across Canada. DYC / JSC offers volunteer opportunities through subcommittees and ad hoc groups that help with projects, activities, and community initiatives.

These groups bring volunteers together around a particular task or goal. Some opportunities involve ongoing collaboration, while others focus on a short-term project. Responsibilities and time commitments depend on the work involved and the opportunities available.

Interested in getting involved?

We would love to hear from you. Tell us about your interests, skills, and availability, or share an idea you would like to contribute. Reach out to our team to learn about current volunteer opportunities and how you can get involved.

Subscribe to our newsletter

Get DYC / JSC news, upcoming events, volunteer opportunities, and community updates in your inbox. Everyone is welcome to subscribe.

Subscribe through Google Groups

Sign in to your Google account, select Join group, and choose Each email to receive every newsletter.

Prefer to subscribe by email?

Send an email from the address you want to subscribe with, then follow Google’s confirmation instructions.

newsletter+subscribe@dycjsc.ca

This is an announcements-only newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the instructions in each email. Subscribing does not make you a registered DYC / JSC member.

About this website

The site provides information about Deaf Youth Canada / Jeunes Sourds du Canada (DYC / JSC), a program operated by the Canadian Association of the Deaf – Association des Sourds du Canada (CAD-ASC). Website information does not replace official bylaws, board decisions, or program-specific documents.

Newsletter subscriptions

Our optional newsletter shares DYC / JSC news, events, volunteer opportunities, and community updates through Google Groups. By completing a subscription, you request these emails at the address you provide. Subscribe only with an address you control; do not enrol someone else without their permission. Visiting this website or sending a general enquiry does not subscribe you.

The newsletter is open to the public. A subscription is separate from DYC / JSC membership, volunteer selection, and event registration. The group is for announcements: only owners and managers can publish. Replies are directed to group managers. Do not use the group to send spam, impersonate others, or share another person’s private information.

You may unsubscribe at any time using the instructions in a newsletter or by emailing newsletter+unsubscribe@dycjsc.ca from your subscribed address. You can also ask info@dycjsc.ca to remove you. Unsubscribing stops future newsletter delivery; it does not cancel a separate membership or event registration.

Google Groups and your email provider handle subscription and delivery. Their terms and privacy practices also apply. Delivery may be affected by your subscription settings, spam filtering, or service interruptions. Our Privacy Policy explains newsletter information handling.

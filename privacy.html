Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers dycjsc.ca, enquiries sent through its contact form or by email, and the DYC / JSC newsletter. Deaf Youth Canada / Jeunes Sourds du Canada (DYC / JSC) is a program operated by the Canadian Association of the Deaf – Association des Sourds du Canada (CAD-ASC). For privacy questions, contact info@dycjsc.ca or write to CAD-ASC, Attention: DYC / JSC privacy enquiries, 251 Bank Street, Suite 606, Ottawa, Ontario K2P 1X3, Canada.

Separate registration processes or external services may provide additional privacy information. This website’s membership page currently opens your email app to ask about joining; it does not itself complete a membership application or collect a payment.

Newsletter information and consent

The DYC / JSC newsletter is managed through Google Groups. When you subscribe, Google Groups processes your email address and may display your name or Google profile information to authorized group administrators. Subscription settings, joining information, and messages you send to managers may also be available to them. DYC / JSC uses this information to deliver newsletters, manage subscriptions, and answer related questions.

Subscribing is optional and requests news, event announcements, volunteer opportunities, and community updates. Simply visiting the website, clicking a sign-up link, or submitting the contact form does not complete a subscription. Follow the joining or email-confirmation steps provided by Google. A newsletter subscription does not constitute DYC / JSC membership.

Subscriber access and visibility

The newsletter group can be found and joined by anyone on the web. Its subscriber list and member email addresses are restricted by group permissions to owners and managers; they are not a public directory. Newsletter conversations are available to group members. Only owners and managers can post, and replies are directed to managers. Authorized Google Workspace administrators and service providers may also have access as part of operating the service.

The website links to Google Groups rather than collecting newsletter addresses in a website form. When you use Google’s services, Google may process account, device, cookie, and technical information under its applicable privacy practices. Email subscription requests are handled by Google and your email provider. These services may process information outside Canada.

Unsubscribing and newsletter records

You can withdraw your newsletter subscription at any time. Use the unsubscribe instructions in an email, send a message to newsletter+unsubscribe@dycjsc.ca from your subscribed address, or contact info@dycjsc.ca for help. You may also leave the group through Google Groups. Changing delivery frequency is different from leaving the group.

Unsubscribing does not erase messages already delivered or automatically delete all related records. Subscription correspondence and service-provider records may be retained separately. Contact info@dycjsc.ca to ask about access, correction, deletion, or retention of your newsletter information. We may need to verify that you control the address concerned. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period for every provider record.

Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. If you email us directly, we receive the information you include. Provide only the information needed for your enquiry; do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Purpose and delivery

Your message is used to respond to your enquiry and manage related follow-up. The contact form sends your information through FormSubmit to info@dycjsc.ca. The relevant DYC / JSC team members and the service providers involved in form delivery and email handling may process this information. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Website hosting and browser storage

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in to GitHub. Hosting and network providers may also process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website. Their practices are described in their own privacy notices.

The website saves your English or French preference in your browser’s local storage under dyc-language. You can remove it by clearing the site’s browser data. This preference stays in that browser and does not require an account. No advertising or analytics scripts have been added to the website. This does not prevent service providers from keeping technical or security logs.

The contact form uses FormSubmit and may require a spam-protection check. Those services may use cookies or process technical information when you submit a message. Emailing info@dycjsc.ca directly avoids FormSubmit, but still involves your and our email providers.

Sensitive information and enquiries about young people

The contact form is not a public comment or profile. Your enquiry may concern membership, volunteering, a camp, or accessibility. Share only what is needed for the initial question. Ask the team how to provide further details before sending medical records, identity documents, or information about another person. Parents or guardians making an enquiry should avoid including unnecessary identifying or sensitive details about a child.

Service-provider processing

The website, form service, spam protection, and email service are separate parts of the delivery process. Their providers may process information in locations outside your province or Canada under their own service arrangements and applicable laws. This policy does not promise Canadian-only storage or a particular provider retention period. Consult the provider policies linked below for more information.

Security and retention

No website or email system can guarantee complete security. Avoid including passwords, banking details, or sensitive documents in the general contact form. If you think you sent information to us by mistake, contact the team promptly and identify the message without repeating the sensitive information.

Enquiry records and provider copies may have different retention periods. This website does not publish a fixed deletion schedule for all records. When asking about retention or deletion, specify the approximate date and subject of your message so the team can identify the relevant records and explain any applicable retention requirements.

Calendar, resource links, and external services

The on-site events calendar loads a public event listing from this website. Selecting a date does not register you for an event or give DYC / JSC access to your personal calendar. Opening View live calendar takes you to Google Calendar. Public event details may be copied or shared by other visitors.

Links to Google Groups, social media, the camp website, and resources take you to separate services. Those services may receive technical information when you visit and may request additional information or a sign-in. Review their privacy notices before providing personal information.

Photos and privacy concerns

Our memories gallery and board page contain publicly visible photographs. If an image of you raises a privacy concern, email the page address, identify the image, and explain your request. Please do not send identity documents with your first message. A website change cannot guarantee removal of copies already saved or shared by other people or services.

Your choices, privacy requests, and concerns

You may browse without contacting us or subscribing. Email info@dycjsc.ca with Privacy request in the subject line to ask about access, correction, deletion, retention, or how your information is used. You may also ask us to stop optional follow-up. Identify the relevant page, service, or approximate message date without repeating sensitive information. The team may need to verify your identity or clarify the records involved.

Deletion requests may be affected by applicable recordkeeping requirements and records held by service providers. We will explain relevant limitations when responding. If a privacy concern remains unresolved, you may contact the privacy regulator with jurisdiction over your concern. Nothing in this policy limits rights available under applicable law.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy when the website or its services change. The date above identifies the latest version. A policy update does not by itself subscribe you to new communications or give consent for a new purpose that requires your consent.
